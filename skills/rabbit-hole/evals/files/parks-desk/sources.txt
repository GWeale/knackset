S1. Bryce Canyon National Park: Bryce Canyon Visitation in 2025.
News release March 17, 2026; page last updated March 20, 2026.
https://home.nps.gov/brca/learn/news/2026-03-17-visitation-stats.htm
The park reports 1,967,367 recreation visits in 2025. It says that earlier counts used total shuttle boardings, which could count someone repeatedly within a visit. Beginning with 2025 data, unique passengers entering via shuttle were counted. The release reports 2,498,075 visits for 2024. It also says that under the former total-boardings method, 2025 would have been reported as 2,437,461 visits, described as a modest 2.4% change. The release mentions a 43-day partial government shutdown and says parks were kept open and accessible whenever possible.
Other 2025 highlights: 11,606,250 visitor hours and 153,741 overnight stays.

S2. NPS Visitor Use Statistics. Page last updated May 16, 2024.
https://www.nps.gov/subjects/socialscience/visitor-use-statistics.htm
A visit is entry onto lands or waters administered by the NPS. Park-specific count procedures govern calculations. Procedure changes can reflect changes in multipliers, detection technology, property or operational capacity. Current and archived procedures can be found through the data portal.

S3. NPS Visitation Numbers. Page last updated March 5, 2025.
https://www.nps.gov/aboutus/visitation-numbers.htm
National recreation visits: 325,498,646 in 2023 and 331,863,358 in 2024.
