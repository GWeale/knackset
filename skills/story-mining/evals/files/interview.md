# Synthetic interview and reporting notes

All names, organizations, quotations, and events are invented for this fixture.
The interviewer and speaker authorize use in the requested local draft.

I01 Interviewer: What started the Saturday repair table?
I02 Mara: I brought a lamp because I liked its crooked neck. Buying a new one
would have been easier. I just wanted that lamp back.
I03 Mara: The first morning, five neighbors came. I had put out twelve chairs.
I kept looking at the empty ones. Then Len asked whether he could hold a screw
tray for someone else. That changed what I thought we were doing.
I04 Interviewer: Was the goal always community building?
I05 Mara: No. I wanted fewer broken things in cupboards. The talking arrived
while our hands were busy. I wouldn't have put that on a poster.
I06 Mara: We still couldn't fix two things that morning. One was a toaster we
couldn't safely open. People stayed anyway. I used to count repairs; now I also
notice who pulls up a chair for someone else.
I07 Interviewer: How often does it happen now?
I08 Mara: The last Saturday of the month, in the library side room. We ask
people to bring one small thing and no promise that we'll rescue it.

N01 Attendance sheet: first morning, five visitors; not five successful repairs.
N02 Repair log: first morning, three items repaired, two not repaired.
N03 No later attendance totals, money savings, or waste measurements were kept.
N04 The lamp is Mara's. Len is a visitor; do not turn him into a co-founder.
N05 Current program name: Saturday Repair Table. There is no paid membership.
