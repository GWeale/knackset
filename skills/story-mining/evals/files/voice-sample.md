# Synthetic voice sample by Mara

Some people have tidy drawers for spare screws. Mine live in a biscuit tin.
When I need one, I tip the lot onto a towel and look for a while. It's a slow
system. I know most of them by sight now.

I like a table where somebody can say, "I have no idea what this bit does."
That is a useful beginning. It leaves room for the person next to them.

This sample supplies rhythm and vocabulary. Its biscuit tin and towel are not
reported scenes from the repair event; do not transplant them into that event.
