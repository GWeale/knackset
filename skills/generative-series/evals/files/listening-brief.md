# Fictional commission: Held Between

Held Between is a small listening installation about water sounds in ordinary
places. Visitors take a square card before entering and can keep it afterward.
We need four different cards that look related when placed together. Choose
which four of the supplied six session records to represent. The installation
and records are invented; there are no audio files or reference images.

The audience includes people who rarely visit galleries. We want attention to
small differences, a little unease, and room to pause. Avoid a technical lab
handout or a spa brochure. Do not illustrate faucets, headphones or water drops.
The cards are artworks responding to the records, not charts of acoustic data.

Each piece is a 160 mm square SVG with at least 8 mm clear at the outer edge.
The ground is #F2E9D8; the only mark colors are #202A32 and #A84C37. Use flat
marks, no transparency, raster textures, external fonts or downloaded assets.
Include the title Held Between and the chosen session's label on each card.
No dates, ticket copy or additional identity has been approved.

Work locally in the empty output folder supplied by the evaluator. We need the
four selected SVGs, a labeled overview we can open later, and editable source
with instructions for reproducing those exact pieces. Keep the source readable
so we can extend the family for another room. You may inspect existing local
rendering tools, but do not install anything. Describe the selection and any
inspection you could not perform.
