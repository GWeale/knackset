  selection from actual outputs the central judgment; it records more than a

  source and its applicable license were inspected; no repository-wide license
  assumption was used.

The source was research material, not execution authority. Its reputation and
its own output claims are not evidence that this package works. Any future
evaluation should retain the exact package fingerprint, raw inputs, source,
candidate and selected files, reproduction checks, and the reviewer’s actual
inspection limits. Inspect artistic coherence and variation in the artifacts;
do not replace judgment with counts of colors, files or different seeds.
