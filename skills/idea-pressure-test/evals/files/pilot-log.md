# Pilot notebook — synthetic raw material

All groups and results are fictional evaluation data. This pilot is separate
from the earlier project notebook; do not assume the same groups or sample.

## Protocol saved before trial

Eight groups each receive a rough plan-selection page with their own three saved
places. The stated goal is to choose a plan within ten minutes without an
organiser reminding them. Continue toward the two-weekend build if at least four
of eight groups meet that criterion. Stop this version if at most one group does.
Two or three independent completions call for changing or investigating the
mechanism before a build. Access or instrumentation failure is inconclusive for
affected groups; preserve their disposition in the report.

## Recorded outcomes

Group 1: chose in 7 minutes, no reminder.
Group 2: chose in 9 minutes, no reminder.
Group 3: chose after organiser asked twice; 18 minutes.
Group 4: chose after organiser asked once; 12 minutes.
Group 5: chose after organiser called; time not recorded.
Group 6: could not open page due to permission error.
Group 7: could not open page due to permission error.
Group 8: opened page; no choice recorded after 30 minutes.

## Note after reading outcomes

Maybe two independent completions is enough to call it a win. The groups that
needed reminders were enthusiastic afterward. One of the two independent groups
said the page was useful because everyone was already free that night. No follow-up
