# Validation and provenance

Status: **draft**. Authored 2026-11-01. The [synthetic cases](evals/evals.json)
are authored inputs and expected behavior, not a passed evaluation suite. No
comparative improvement or live validation is claimed. Structural validation
cannot establish that the skill improves judgment or routes correctly.

This original procedure combines a fair case for an idea, an important unresolved
claim, rival explanations, and an affordable test with criteria set before its
results. It deliberately keeps creative and learning goals distinct from claims
about commercial demand. No upstream prose or implementation files were copied.

The [MIT license](https://github.com/garrytan/gstack/blob/a563f476793b61edbb064b853d52cb125a1996c4/LICENSE) and [third-party notices](https://github.com/garrytan/gstack/blob/a563f476793b61edbb064b853d52cb125a1996c4/NOTICE.md) were inspected; no office-hours exception was listed.
