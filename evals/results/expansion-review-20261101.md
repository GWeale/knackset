# Expansion and refactor review, November 1, 2026

Three new original draft methods, three revised utility methods and concrete
outputs for inspection. The [examples](../../examples/expansion/README.md) and
[older-skill review](../../docs/refactor-review-20261101.md) keep the work visible.
This is a scoped authoring/review pass, not a performance comparison or proof that
the collection is the best available.

## Trial design

Each of three new methods had one fresh-context task. Two revised methods also
had separate fresh-context artifact boundaries. Three existing sandbox scenarios
were rerun in three other fresh contexts. A ninth fresh context received only the
25 names/descriptions and 27 anonymous routing tasks. After review identified
missing positive discovery cases, a tenth fresh context received the complete
expanded 30-task packet. Authoring agents and the
read-only old-method audit were separate from task-performing agents. No helper
agent saw the expected outputs or judge criteria for its execution/selection task.

The task agents used native Codex in T3 Code, `gpt-6.1-sol` with inherited `xhigh`
reasoning. Exact harness build, token totals and incremental billing cost were not
captured. Execution dates are November 2 UTC, November 1 in America/Los_Angeles.
No network, browser/GUI, live account, installation or publication was used by the
execution agents. Source researchers used primary public HTTP material. No
upstream prose, code, artwork or fonts were bundled.

The [artifact manifest](expansion-artifacts-20261101.json) identifies loaded skill
text, raw inputs and published outputs. New-package validation notes were updated
after the trials; loaded entrypoints and inputs are unchanged. These artifact
reviews do not claim a promoted whole-package fingerprint. The three utility
retests use the stricter initialization/export bindings described below.
