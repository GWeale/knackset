# Field-kit artifact review — November 1, 2026

Six actual outputs, one supplied-input trial for each new skill. All six packages
remain **draft**. These examples show what was made and what was inspected; they
do not establish that a skill improves an agent, transfers to other tasks, or has
passed its full authored case set. [Open the artifacts](../../examples/field-kit/README.md).

## Setup and evidence

Execution took place through native Codex agents in T3 Code, using
`gpt-6.1-sol` with the parent's `xhigh` reasoning setting. Exact harness build,
token usage and billing cost were not captured. Three fresh contexts produced
Reference Remix, Story Mining and Idea Pressure Test; those lanes then produced
Learn by Making, Rabbit Hole and Demo Director respectively. The second wave
retained earlier context. There were not six independent fresh contexts.

Agents received the selected `SKILL.md`, the named raw inputs and an artifact
brief. They were told not to read expected outputs, validation notes, other trial
outputs, tests or grading code. This is a supplied-material exercise, not a live
research or host-discovery test. No trial agent used a browser, network, account,
installer, external publication or helper agent.

The [manifest](field-kit-artifacts-20261101.json) records the exact skill/input
hashes and published artifact hashes. Package validation notes were updated after
the trials; the skill bodies and loaded inputs are unchanged. No whole-package
fingerprint claim or promoted status is based on these examples.

The coordinator reviewed all outputs against their source material. This review
was unblinded and used the judgments below; it is not a pre-registered quantitative
study. An independent reader reviewed the actual six skill bodies, cases,
fixtures, source shelf and discovery docs. That reader did not grade the artwork.

## Production limits and retained imperfections

The demo is prepared source, not finished video. Installed video routes checked
by the author were unavailable, and the active browser hold was respected. Its
simulated-DOM check cannot establish legibility, frame pacing or real browser
behavior. A caption-verification command initially failed because its timestamp
parser assumed three colon-separated fields; the parser was corrected for valid
`MM:SS.mmm` cues before the final check passed. This was a check defect, not a
concealed failed product interaction.

The poster was tried from interpreted textual studies. A future visual-reference
trial should supply actual images and inspect how the agent reads their formal
decisions. The worked poster is useful, but it cannot answer that question.

The product brief proposes a deliberately tiny trial with judgment-based cutoffs.
It is a decision aid, not evidence of demand. The newsletter is a plausible draft;
only its fictional sources were checked. The investigation's source packet cannot
resolve causation. The learning graphic demonstrates the rule without observing
whether anybody learned it.

Published outputs are retained as produced, except that learning run commands
were made repository-relative, a clearly labeled parent follow-up was added, and
blank-line whitespace in the SVG generator and output was removed. The parent
reran the published script; its SVG render is pixel-identical to the trial output.
PNG previews, the example index and parent review notes were created separately.
Original replies and local verification material remain private; no private
history or third-party asset is included in public examples.

## Review and repository checks

The independent review found one material onboarding defect: the reproducible
install page still selected Sheets at an older revision. The selected-package
recipe now defaults to Rabbit Hole at a revision containing the new methods.
The [copy check](field-kit-install-20261101.json) executed the documented Python
block twice for each of the six packages, with matching manifests and overwrite
refusal. It did not test host activation or the optional CLI. No
other material scope, routing or authorization defect was found. The reviewer
identified the textual-reference coverage gap above as an honest limitation.

The combined repository's 120 unit tests passed after integrating all six skills.
These test helper behavior and integrity, not the quality of these six artifacts.
Structural validation, generated-catalog freshness and local-link/whitespace
checks are recorded with the final change. Existing utility benchmark records
are preserved and are not used to validate this new direction.
