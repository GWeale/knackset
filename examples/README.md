# Work you can inspect

The earlier examples are actual agent outputs from fictional raw material. They
show what was made, the decisions behind it and what was checked. They are initial trials,
without comparison baselines or a claim of general effectiveness.

- [The first field kit](field-kit/README.md) includes a poster, cinema investigation,
  newsletter piece, product decision brief, curve experiment and prepared demo.
- [The expansion](expansion/README.md) includes a generative edition, a recovered
  design decision and a printable label proof. It also shows an existing method
  amended from session evidence and a reduced reproduction from supplied records.
- [Actual launch work](launch-study/README.md) contains three fresh matched pairs,
  with and without the selected method, and an independent anonymous review.
  [The 80-second captioned preview](creative-preview/README.md) presents saved outputs.
- [Field Notes](field-notes/README.md) adds two matched repetitions of three
  source-backed tasks and three fresh boundary responses. The
  [case study](field-notes/CASE-STUDY.md) uses actual NPS figures and USGS imagery
  under authored work requests; it is not an outside-customer story.

Sources and instructions are separate from the outputs. Review records distinguish
agent execution, source-based reasoning, structural checks and visual inspection.
The creative and investigative field-kit packages remain draft.
