## Calculation

Inputs below are **recreation visits** from S1. Both year-to-year percentage declines use **2024’s 2,498,075 visits** as denominator.

```text
Published gap       = 2,498,075 − 1,967,367 = 530,708
Published decline   = 530,708 / 2,498,075 × 100 = 21.2446784%
Former-method gap   = 2,498,075 − 2,437,461 = 60,614
Former-method drop  = 60,614 / 2,498,075 × 100 = 2.4264283%
Method difference   = 2,437,461 − 1,967,367 = 470,094
Gap decomposition   = 60,614 + 470,094 = 530,708
Method share of gap = 470,094 / 530,708 × 100 = 88.5786534%
```

The method difference is 18.8183 percentage points of the 2024 baseline. Its 88.6% share of the reported gap depends on the park’s counterfactual; it is not a causal estimate of individual travel decisions.
