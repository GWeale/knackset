# Field Notes — a fold in the field

`color.svg` and `ink.svg` are finished A5 portrait masters (148 × 210 mm). All text and geometry remain editable. `color-preview.png` and `ink-preview.png` are opaque 300 dpi local renders (1748 × 2480 px). Print the SVG at 100% / actual size on white paper.

The governing idea is a field of repeated boundaries that compresses, opens, and breaks once. The large serif invitation leads into the specimen; three quiet writing areas follow. A broad band catches the first look; its narrow interruption and the unevenly spaced cross marks reward another. Each prompt has two 124 mm rules, 7 mm apart, with 10 mm from prompt baseline to first rule. Type uses the installed DejaVu Serif and DejaVu Sans families.

I compared a narrow side specimen with a writing column, scattered islands between the prompts, and the selected specimen above three full-width responses. The selected arrangement gives everyone the same observation target without narrowing the writing area. The color edition uses deep teal, clay and one pale green field; the ink edition keeps the same geometry and hierarchy through black line weights and white space. White denotes unprinted paper, not a second ink.

## What the supplied images contributed

- **Earth Selfie:** I inspected the dark sea against a detailed, irregular land edge. The useful mechanism is a boundary that can change the reading of a form. I borrowed that invitation to reconsider, excluding the recognizable face/coastline silhouette.
- **Lena Delta:** I inspected the fan of dense channels and the dark intervals that separate them. Compressed spacing contributes visual density here, without copying the radial drainage network, source frame, lettering or mark.
- **Meandering Mississippi:** I inspected the repeated curves amid smaller patch-like detail. Recurrence with local change informed the varying contour spacing and single broken band; no river or cutoff shape was traced.

The references disagree in their density and color intensity. The worksheet gives restraint control: an open page and limited palette contain the complexity in one specimen. It deliberately excludes photographic texture, map labels, agency marks and a supposed scientific color key. Rights and source descriptions come from the supplied `sources.txt` (dated 2026-11-02); no web verification was performed. This is independent original vector work, with no agency endorsement.

## Observed checks and gaps

Both final layouts were rendered locally with installed librsvg and visually inspected at a 150 dpi A5 review size. The inspection caught and corrected a transparent preview background and an incorrectly placed band interruption. The final master has explicit white paper. Structural checks passed for exact approved visible text, physical dimensions, editable text, no image nodes/external links, and a minimum type size of 10.20 pt. Rendered text bounds fit the page margins. The ink master specifies only black, white and no paint, with no transparency, gray or filters. `checks.json` records the measured checks. All supplied inputs were preserved; image hashes match their manifest.

No physical printer proof or alternate-editor opening was performed. Installed-font rendering was verified here; another machine needs those fonts to retain exact line metrics. No claim of scientific measurement, accessibility certification or print-shop color proofing is made.
