# Field Notes — open bend

Finished files: `color.svg` and `ink.svg`. Both are editable A5 portrait sources (148 × 210 mm). The accompanying PNGs are white-backed local previews at 559 × 794 px, approximately 96 dpi at A5. `checks.json` records the observed checks and supplied-file hashes.

The direction puts a quiet, repeated bend beside a two-line title, then gives the rest of the page to observation. The first reading finds the outer edge; the second finds tighter turns and changes in spacing. Six original Bézier paths make the mark. Text remains text, and every rule and contour remains an individually editable vector.

The inspected references contributed mechanisms rather than shapes:

- **Earth Selfie:** the decisive edge between detailed land and a nearly empty dark field gives a clear figure and ground. Borrow the breathing room, not its recognizable face/coastline.
- **Lena Delta:** repetition becomes branching density. Borrow the changing intervals and nested rhythm, while withholding its busy all-over texture and radial geography.
- **Meandering Mississippi:** successive bends and adjacent traces reward following a line slowly. Borrow that delayed discovery, not the river's course, field pattern, source lettering or agency mark.

Two composition routes were considered: a branching field of small marks throughout the sheet, and one open bend above generous writing areas. The open bend wins because the dense alternative would compete with handwritten observations. The serif title and compact sans-serif identity keep it a portable practice sheet. No map, photograph, agency identity or scientific color key is used.

The color edition pairs deep teal with terracotta and citron. These are expressive choices, not interpretations of satellite bands. The ink edition uses only solid black strokes/text and unprinted white; its accent weights were adjusted to preserve hierarchy without gray or transparency.

Each prompt has three 124 mm writing rules, 8 mm apart. The approved visible words are unchanged. All required text is at least 10.205 pt. Installed Noto Serif and Noto Sans were used, with generic fallbacks; fonts are not embedded.

Checks passed: exact copy, dimensions, minimum type size, safe rendered bounds, editable text, no image nodes/external references, black-only ink paints, and unchanged input hashes. Both current previews were visually inspected for legibility, clipping and writing space. References are credited to USGS EROS using the supplied `sources.txt` public-domain statements; no network verification was performed.

Remaining limits: no physical print/pen test, printer scaling/color proof or cross-editor font-substitution test. Print at 100% / actual size. The method's optional status resource was not supplied and was not consulted.
