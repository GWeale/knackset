# Source trail

Prepared 2026-11-02 from the supplied `parks-desk/sources.txt` and `counts.csv` only. These are bounded transcriptions of NPS material, retrieved on that date; no live pages, archived counting procedures or independent attendance records were checked. `request.txt` supplies the editorial assignment, not evidence.

| Source | Supplied dates | Use and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [S1 — Bryce Canyon Visitation in 2025](https://home.nps.gov/brca/learn/news/2026-03-17-visitation-stats.htm) | Released 2026-03-17; updated 2026-03-20 | All Bryce totals, shuttle-method change, former-method 2025 estimate and shutdown context. The estimate comes from the park; the pack does not reproduce its derivation. The release’s “2.4% change” is a decline when calculated from its figures. |
| [S2 — NPS Visitor Use Statistics](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/socialscience/visitor-use-statistics.htm) | Updated 2024-05-16 | Visit definition and park-specific counting procedures. Supports distinguishing visits from annual unique visitors; does not audit Bryce’s counts. |
| [S3 — NPS Visitation Numbers](https://www.nps.gov/aboutus/visitation-numbers.htm) | Updated 2025-03-05 | National totals for 2023 and 2024 only. Not used to explain Bryce’s 2025 change: no national 2025 comparator is supplied. |

`counts.csv` repeats the numeric series attributed to S1/S3; it is not a separate corroborating source. S1’s visitor hours and overnight stays are different measures and cannot supply a missing annual unique-visitor count.

The key calculation is reproduced in [calculation.md](calculation.md). It decomposes the published gap using S1’s counterfactual; it is not a causal estimate of the shutdown or of individual travel choices. Real gaps: no revised-method 2024 count, full count-procedure audit, quantified shutdown impact or evidence explaining the remaining decline. The article is an editorial synthesis, not live validation of the counting system.
