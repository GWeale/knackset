# The calculation that matters

All inputs are recreation visits from S1, transcribed in `parks-desk/counts.csv`:

| Symbol | Series | Visits |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| A | 2024 reported, earlier method | 2,498,075 |
| B | 2025 reported, refined method | 1,967,367 |
| C | 2025 park-stated former-method counterfactual | 2,437,461 |

**Published-total decline:**

`A − B = 530,708`

`(A − B) / A × 100 = 21.24467840% ≈ 21.2%`

**Comparison using the former method for both years:**

`A − C = 60,614`

`(A − C) / A × 100 = 2.42642835% ≈ 2.4%`

**Contribution of the 2025 method difference to the published gap:**

`C − B = 470,094`

`530,708 = 60,614 + 470,094`

`(C − B) / (A − B) × 100 = 88.57865342% ≈ 88.6%`

Both annual decline percentages use the **2024 total** as denominator. Their difference is **18.81825005 percentage points**, not 18.8 percent. The 88.6% share instead uses the **530,708-visit published gap** as denominator.

This is an exact arithmetic decomposition of the supplied figures. C is the park’s estimate under the former method, not another observed published count. The calculation does not identify the cause of the remaining decline, measure annual unique visitors, or reconstruct 2024 under the refined method.
