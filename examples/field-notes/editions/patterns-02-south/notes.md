# Field Notes

Two original, editable A5 portrait worksheets: `color.svg` and `ink.svg`. Local viewing proofs are `color.png` and `ink.png`.

The visual direction is **edge → rhythm → branching**. Three abstract specimens reinterpret the supplied references' contrasting boundaries, repeated meanders, and branching structure. All geometry was authored independently; no photograph, distinctive traced outline, source lettering, or agency mark appears in the artwork. The forest green and clay palette is an aesthetic choice with no scientific color key.

Both editions share their layout and live text. DejaVu Serif supplies the title; DejaVu Sans supplies the identity, prompts, and footer. Copy ranges from 10 to 23.5 pt. Each prompt has two 122 mm writing rules at 12 mm spacing. The pages are 148 × 210 mm, with 13 mm side margins and no bleed. Print at 100% / actual size on A5. Fonts remain editable and require the same installed fonts for identical rendering elsewhere.

The supplied shelf identifies *Earth Selfie*, *Lena Delta*, and *Meandering Mississippi* as USGS public-domain references, checked 2026-11-02. That supplied rights record was used without network access. This is independent work with no agency endorsement.

**Observed checks:** both previews rendered with installed librsvg/Cairo at 1119 × 1587 px and were visually inspected for readable copy, page edges, spacing, and writing room. XML checks passed for physical dimensions, exact approved text, minimum type size, absence of image nodes, and black/white-only ink paints without opacity or filters. Input SHA-256 hashes remained unchanged. `checks.json` records those checks.

**Gaps:** no physical print or handwriting trial; no other SVG editor, font-substitution, or printer behavior was tested. Raster preview antialiasing introduces screen grays; the ink SVG itself declares only black and white.
