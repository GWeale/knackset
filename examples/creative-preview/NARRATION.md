# Narration for the captioned preview

The GIF has six timed frames totaling 80 seconds. This narration is a suggested
read, not recorded audio or observed human timing.

**0–8 seconds.** Knackset is a collection of methods for curious people who make
things. Follow a question. Make something worth keeping.

**8–22 seconds.** Start with actual material: a claim with conflicting sources,
references that need an original direction, or existing work that has stopped
moving. We chose Rabbit Hole, Reference Remix and Unstick Project for this preview.

**22–40 seconds.** Here is one real launch brief, completed twice in fresh contexts.
The left card was made without the Knackset skill. The right used Reference Remix.
You can inspect both editable sources, their decisions and the comparison review.

**40–56 seconds.** The investigation asks what our evidence actually measures.
Helper tests check the tools. The old mail comparison checks its own tasks. Those
numbers cannot tell you whether the creative methods improve your work.

**56–70 seconds.** The stalled-launch task produced a local demo packet. An actual
artifact, source trail and first task can be ready while outside feedback remains
open. These three pairs are a small pilot on one project's launch work.

**70–80 seconds.** Give one method a real job for twenty minutes. Tell us what you
kept, fixed or discarded. The test guide and examples are linked from the README.
