# Night Orchard — decisive choices

**Chosen direction: a tree you can listen around.** A large, plainspoken title introduces a quiet night field. One irregular orchard crown, a dotted walking route and three groups of listening strokes turn “observant and inviting” into visible relationships: stillness at the centre, human arrivals at its edges. The warm, practical information strip supplies an easy way into the event.

## The references are textual studies

I read the three supplied descriptions, not reference images. Their imaginary lettering, insignia and other distinctive content are not assets for this poster.

- **R2 controls the composition.** Its isolated form and quiet field reward attention without filling every gap. Pale green contour lines against deep green-black make the orchard feel discoverable. The new subject is an open orchard crown and walking route, rather than the study’s insect observation sheet. I excluded peripheral scientific annotations that could make first-time visitors feel examined or excluded.
- **R3 challenges that stillness.** Unequal groups of short warm strokes mark separate listening stops. Their different counts make the route feel shared without portraits or literal handwriting. The social rhythm serves an evening of sound installations and conversations; no choir notation, baseline or page texture is reproduced.
- **R1 defines a boundary.** Reliable hierarchy is useful for a date and venue, but its dominant stencils, tan surface and diagonal inspection slash would make the event read as freight or industrial nightlife. None of those treatments appears. This reference does not need a visible fragment in the finished work.

## Two ways to organize it

1. **Walking score:** a route crosses the entire page, with title, time and venue entering along it. This would foreground sound and participation, but fragment the reading order and make practical details harder to find.
2. **Quiet field / open crown — selected:** title first, invitation second, a spare central image, then a clear information strip. It better supports curious adults who do not already identify with science: the facts are easy, and the image offers something to notice without demanding specialist knowledge.

The title is deliberately large and unadorned. A small footer and serif display lettering could have pushed this toward luxury wellness; cartoon fruit and cheerful illustrated people could have pushed it toward a farm day. Both are excluded. The original crown’s uneven gaps and the changing number of listening strokes supply the nocturnal uncertainty and social life.
