# First independent feedback round

Status: recruiting. No independent human reports have been collected by this
launch task. The first-round target is five people trying one creative method
on a real task. Agent evaluations do not count toward that target.

Use the [test packet and invitation](try-the-field-kit.md), then submit the
[field-test form](https://github.com/GWeale/knackset/issues/new?template=field-test.yml).
No direct invitations have been sent by this task. Recruitment needs people who
actually want to try the work; this record does not invent recipients or responses.

## Review each report

1. Confirm the skill revision, agent/model and whether loading was observed.
2. Inspect the shareable artifact or reduced reproduction. Separate a tool or
   loading failure from an instruction or output-quality failure.
3. Record what the tester kept, repaired or discarded. Describe the consequential
   mistake and evidence instead of averaging unrelated satisfaction scores.
4. Reproduce a supported defect, repair the narrow cause and ask whether the same
   task now works. Keep the original failure and link the retest.

Record report links and dispositions here once they exist. Ask permission before
quoting private feedback or reusing someone's work in a demo. The first-round
review stays open until five inspectable independent reports are available.
